Be a wildlife helper!

Animals need our help. Here are some easy things you can do today.

  • 🦔

    Leave a gap in the fence

    Hedgehogs walk a long way at night. A hole at the bottom of the fence lets them move between gardens.

  • 🍃

    Leave a messy corner

    Piles of leaves and long grass give small animals a place to hide and sleep.

  • 🌳

    Plant a tree

    Trees with seeds and nuts give squirrels food, especially in autumn.

  • 🥣

    Never give wild animals milk

    Milk can make wild animals very ill. Ask a grown-up to call a wildlife rescue if you find an animal that needs help.
