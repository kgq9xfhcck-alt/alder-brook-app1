Helping Britain's wild animals come home

Alder Brook Wildlife Centre rescues sick, injured and orphaned wildlife. We rehabilitate them and return them to the wild, or give a lifelong home to those who could not survive outside.

Meet our animals

Our conservation work

Every animal that comes to Alder Brook follows the same path, with one aim: getting it back where it belongs.

  1. 1. Rescue

    We take in sick, injured and orphaned British wildlife brought to us by members of the public and local vets.

  2. 2. Rehabilitate

    We treat injuries, hand-rear young animals and build up their strength in quiet enclosures, away from people.

  3. 3. Release

    When an animal is healthy and can fend for itself, we return it to a suitable habitat, as close as possible to where it was found.

  4. 4. A home for life

    Animals that could not survive in the wild stay with us for life, and help visitors learn about British wildlife.

Why it matters

Many British species are in trouble. Hedgehog numbers have fallen by more than 25% in the last decade, and red squirrels have declined more than almost any other UK mammal.
